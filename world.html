WØRLD

Brief Overview

WØRLD (ordinarily spelled "WORLD") is a Science Fantasy Media Franchise with a Futuristic Spacefaring World Setting and Infinite Scalability.

The Areas of Focus for Product Development include:

  • Role-Playing Game (RPG)
  • Novel
  • Film
  • Merchandise

Features

WØRLD is franchise of Media Products for people with individually different or multiple interests at the same time.

  • RPG
    • Digital Edition — Explore a Fantasy Universe with Infinite Scalability on a PC or Mobile Web Browser with no Download or Installation requirement.
    • Physical Edition — Develop a Fantasy Universe in the Physical World alongside Friends that could span and scale across multiple play sessions.
  • Novel — Stories of the Science Fantasy Universe that will be told through Digital eBook and Physical Trade Paperback editions.
  • Film — Stories of the Science Fantasy Universe that will be told through Subscription Video On Demand (SVOD).
  • Merchandise — Physical Gifts (e.g. Coaster, Mug, Poster) with the Science Fantasy Universe Brand.

Outlook

Developmental State - Optimistic Feasibility and Viability of the Science Fantasy Media Franchise provide strong justification for the commencement and sustenance of Development Activity for the underlying Products.
